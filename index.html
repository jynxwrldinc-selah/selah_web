The Program

One eight-week journey.
Two tracks.

Every member is onboarded and assigned a track — Brotherhood for men, Sisterhood for women — then works the same daily rhythm together with a small circle of people doing it alongside them.

  1. 01

    Daily scripture & devotionals

    A short passage and a guided devotional each morning, building week over week toward a clear theme.

  2. 02

    Prayer

    Guided and free-form prayer built into the daily loop, not bolted on as an afterthought.

  3. 03

    Daily tasks

    Concrete, assigned actions for the day — not just content to read and forget.

  4. 04

    Brotherhood & Sisterhood

    An accountability circle and community feed of people working the same week you are.

  5. 05

    Progress you can see

    Streaks, milestones, and a growth score that reflects consistency, not just activity.

  6. 06

    Hey Selah

    A voice-guided assistant for hands-free navigation through your daily program.

Built with care

Faith-first, and honest about what that requires.

Selah handles prayer requests, check-ins, and moments people don't share anywhere else. We designed the app around that trust from the start — not as a policy added after the fact.

  • 01
    Private by design Your content stays yours. Row-level access controls mean no one else's account can read it — not even by accident.
  • 02
    Safety-first crisis path If you're in crisis, Selah routes you toward real help first — that path is never simplified away to make the app feel smoother.
  • 03
    You can leave, cleanly Delete your account and your data from inside the app, any time. See our deletion instructions.
