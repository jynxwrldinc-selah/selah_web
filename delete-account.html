Delete Your Account

You can delete your Selah account and its data at any time, from inside the app.

In the app

1

Open Selah and go to Settings.

2

Tap Account, then Delete Account.

3

Confirm the deletion when prompted.

This step is final — deletion cannot be undone from within the app.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes your profile, program progress, prayer and journal content, check-in history, and community posts associated with your account. Content you shared with an accountability circle or the community feed is removed from your profile, though copies visible to other members as part of a shared conversation may persist in their view.

We retain a limited record where required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes, and your deletion request is processed within DELETION TIMEFRAME. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can't access the app?

If you're unable to sign in or the in-app option isn't working, email support@selah.app from the address on your account and ask us to delete your account. We'll verify your identity and confirm once it's done.
