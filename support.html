Billing & subscriptions

All payments run through the Apple App Store. To manage, cancel, or request a refund for a subscription, go to your iPhone's Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions, or visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Safety or crisis concern

Selah is not a monitored emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. You can also call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or text HOME to 741741 for the Crisis Text Line — rather than waiting on a reply here.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Download Selah once it's live on the App Store, create an account, and complete onboarding — you'll be assigned a track (Brotherhood or Sisterhood) and your first week of the program will unlock.

I'm having trouble signing in.

Confirm you're using the email address you signed up with. If you still can't get in, email support@theselahbibleapp.com from that same address and we'll help you regain access.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple. Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your device, select Selah, and choose Cancel. Cancelling stops future renewals; it does not retroactively refund the current period.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account and its data directly in the app. See Delete Your Account for step-by-step instructions.

How do I report inappropriate content in my circle or the community feed?

Use the report option on the post or message, or email support@theselahbibleapp.com with a description and, if possible, a screenshot. We review reports and take action under our community conduct terms.
